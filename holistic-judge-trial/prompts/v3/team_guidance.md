The photo should be taken from the front or side of the people, not from behind them. If the main people in the frame are seen mainly from behind (their backs towards the camera), the rules about camera angle and the volunteer's back are normally not met (FAIL).
Exception: a back view of volunteers is fine, and must not count against the photo, when ALL of these are true: (1) the volunteers are wearing the dress code, (2) the NGO's logo and name are visible on the back of their clothing, and (3) the beneficiaries are clearly visible. In that case the volunteers' faces do not need to be visible. If any one of the three conditions is missing, the guidance above applies in full.
