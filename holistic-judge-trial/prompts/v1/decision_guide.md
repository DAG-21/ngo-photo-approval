## How to decide

- Read every rule above, then look at the photograph as a whole and judge how well it meets the rules, the way an experienced reviewer would.
- None of the rules is marked mandatory in the client's document, so no single rule decides the outcome on its own. Weigh how serious each shortfall is.
- A rule that depends on something that is not in the photograph (for example, there is no banner) does not count against it.
- Base every statement on what is visible. If you genuinely cannot tell, say so (cannot_judge) instead of guessing.
