## Terms and how to answer

- Volunteer: a person delivering the seva (serving food, teaching, treating, caring for animals). Volunteers usually wear white and may wear an NGO ID card or branded clothing.
- Beneficiary: a person receiving the seva. Decide this from the person's role in the activity only, never from their appearance. People who are neither (passers-by, onlookers) are neither.
- Every question is worded so that YES means the requirement is met and NO means it is not met.
- Answer with one of: YES, NO, CANNOT_TELL, NOT_APPLICABLE.
  - Questions that ask whether something is present (for example "Is at least one beneficiary visible?") are answered YES or NO, never NOT_APPLICABLE.
  - NOT_APPLICABLE only when the condition in brackets after the question is true (for example, no banner is present).
  - CANNOT_TELL when the photograph does not show enough to answer (too small, too dark, hidden). Do not guess.
  - Do not answer NO merely because something is absent unless the question is about that very thing.
- Base every answer on what is visible. Answer each question on its own and do not let one answer change another.
- For every NO or CANNOT_TELL, give a short evidence phrase (at most 15 words) saying what you see. For YES and NOT_APPLICABLE leave the evidence empty.
