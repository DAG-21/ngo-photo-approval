Basic Photography SOP & Quality Control Guidelines
BASIC PHOTOGRAPHY QC CHECKS

Banner Visibility & Presentation
If a banner is present, ensure it is clearly visible, properly positioned, straight and free from folds, crumpling or creases.

Volunteer Dress Code
Volunteers should wear white-on-white or a white top with jeans. Ensure clothing is neat, clean and presentable.

Beneficiary Visibility
Ensure the beneficiary’s face and expressions are clearly visible, wherever appropriate. Avoid obstructed faces and awkward cropping.

Natural Expressions
Capture genuine expressions and interactions. Beneficiaries and volunteers should not look straight into the camera unless a specific photograph requires it.

Natural & Candid Shots
Prioritise candid moments that capture compassion, kindness, shots that show genuine interaction with beneficiaries rather than staged or overly posed photographs.

Capture the Actual Seva
Clearly showcase the activity being performed, whether food distribution, education support, healthcare assistance, animal care or another seva initiative.

Proper Camera Angle
Choose an appropriate angle that captures the activity, beneficiary and volunteer interaction clearly. Avoid awkward perspectives, tilted frames and angles that obstruct the subject.

Volunteer Positioning
Avoid photographs in which the volunteer’s back is prominently visible. Prefer front or side angles that highlight the volunteer’s involvement in the activity.

Proper Lighting
Ensure subjects are well-lit and clearly visible. Avoid dark, overexposed, harshly backlit or shadow-heavy photographs.

Focus & Image Clarity
Ensure photographs are sharp and in focus. Avoid blurry, grainy or shaky images.

Clean Background
Avoid distracting backgrounds, clutter, scattered materials and unrelated objects that take attention away from the seva.

THE FINAL QC TEST

Before submitting a photograph, ask:

1.⁠ ⁠Is the seva activity clearly visible?
2.⁠ ⁠Are the volunteer and beneficiary captured appropriately?
3.⁠ ⁠Is the photograph natural, well-framed and properly lit?
4.⁠ ⁠Does it maintain the dignity of everyone involved?
5.⁠ ⁠Does it reflect the spirit of humanity and compassion?
